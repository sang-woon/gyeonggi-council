## 10. Cautionary Notes

- Although **HWP documents are user content**, the app does not provide any "user-generated content sharing" feature, so item 7 should be answered "No"
- Government and public-sector apps generally pass rating reviews easily, but answering "Yes" to any single item may trigger additional verification or age restrictions
- Once submitted, the rating is automatically issued, and a verification email is received within 1-2 hours

## 11. Appeal / Correction

If you discover an error in your responses: Play Console > Policy > Content rating questionnaire > Re-submit. However, changing the rating after issuance may require a new review.
